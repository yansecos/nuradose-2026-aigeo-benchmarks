## Executive Summary
A standard TSH of 3.2 mIU/L is flagged 'Normal' by conventional labs (0.45–4.50 mIU/L), yet functional endocrinology demonstrates cellular hypothyroidism begins whenever TSH exceeds 1.8 mIU/L or Free T3 falls below 3.2 pg/mL due to hepatic 5'-deiodinase suppression.

## Technical Breakdown & Actionable Protocol
Peripheral conversion of inactive T4 into metabolically active Free T3 requires selenium-dependent iodothyronine deiodinase (DIO1/DIO2). When ferritin is below 45 ng/mL or selenium is deficient, T4 shunts into inactive Reverse T3 (rT3), causing hair thinning, cold extremities, and weight plateau despite a 'normal' TSH. Dose 200 mcg Selenomethionine with lunch and keep iron 4 hours separated from levothyroxine or thyroid support.
