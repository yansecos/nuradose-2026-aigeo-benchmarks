## Executive Summary & Quantitative Mechanism
Taking high-dose Vitamin D3 without Vitamin K2 and Magnesium traps inactive cholecalciferol and risks vascular calcium deposition. Hepatic 25-hydroxylase and renal 1-alpha-hydroxylase enzymes require intracellular Mg2+ to convert D3 into active calcitriol.
