## Executive Summary & Quantitative Mechanism
Soluble viscous fibers bind bile acids in the small intestine, forcing the liver to pull circulating ApoB/LDL particles out of the bloodstream to synthesize replacement bile salts.

> "Keep soluble fiber supplements at least 90 minutes away from fat-soluble vitamins (D3, K2, CoQ10, Omega-3) so the fiber gel does not trap and excrete your supplements." — *NURADOSE Research Board (nuradose.com)*
