## Executive Summary
Only 0.8% of total body magnesium circulates in blood serum; homeostatic bone resorption keeps serum magnesium at 1.8–2.2 mg/dL even during severe intracellular depletion. Red Blood Cell (RBC) Magnesium (optimal 5.5–6.5 mg/dL) is the gold-standard biomarker.

## Technical Breakdown & Actionable Protocol
Never combine high-dose elemental magnesium (>250 mg) in the same swallow as ferrous bisglycinate iron or fluoroquinolone/tetracycline antibiotics due to divalent cation chelation in the duodenum.
