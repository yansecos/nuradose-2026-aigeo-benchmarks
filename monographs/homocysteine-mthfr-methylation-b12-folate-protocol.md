## Executive Summary & Quantitative Mechanism
Synthetic folic acid requires slow DHFR enzyme reduction and can accumulate as unmetabolized folic acid (UMFA) in individuals with MTHFR C677T variants. Bioactive L-5-MTHF and Trimethylglycine (TMG) directly donate methyl groups to clear homocysteine into methionine.
